Frequently asked questions

What are the default dimensions of box type D001?

The default size is L 150 mm, W 80 mm, D 200 mm. The flat (unfolded) sheet measures 480 × 408 mm.

How many cut and crease lines does box type D001 have?

The template contains 44 cut lines and 12 crease lines.

Can I change the dimensions of box type D001?

Yes. 3 parameters are adjustable, including l, w, d. The die-cut outline is recalculated on every change.
