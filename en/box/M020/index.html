Frequently asked questions

What are the default dimensions of box type M020?

The default size is L 150 mm, W 120 mm, D 40 mm. The flat (unfolded) sheet measures 258 × 228 mm.

How many cut and crease lines does box type M020 have?

The template contains 16 cut lines and 8 crease lines.

Can I change the dimensions of box type M020?

Yes. 3 parameters are adjustable, including l, w, d. The die-cut outline is recalculated on every change.

Which export formats are supported?

SVG, DXF (R12), PDF and PNG. Cut and crease lines are kept as separate layers in SVG.
