Frequently asked questions

What are the default dimensions of box type E001?

The default size is L 300 mm, W 200 mm, D 100 mm. The flat (unfolded) sheet measures 715 × 400 mm.

How many cut and crease lines does box type E001 have?

The template contains 40 cut lines and 16 crease lines.

Can I change the dimensions of box type E001?

Yes. 4 parameters are adjustable, including l, w, d, sty1. The die-cut outline is recalculated on every change.

Which export formats are supported?

SVG, DXF (R12), PDF and PNG. Cut and crease lines are kept as separate layers in SVG.
