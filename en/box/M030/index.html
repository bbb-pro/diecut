Frequently asked questions

What are the default dimensions of box type M030?

The default size is L 200 mm, W 100 mm, D 50 mm. The flat (unfolded) sheet measures 300 × 203 mm.

How many cut and crease lines does box type M030 have?

The template contains 40 cut lines and 6 crease lines.

Can I change the dimensions of box type M030?

Yes. 3 parameters are adjustable, including l, w, d. The die-cut outline is recalculated on every change.
