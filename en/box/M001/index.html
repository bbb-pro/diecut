Frequently asked questions

What are the default dimensions of box type M001?

The default size is L 150 mm, W 120 mm. The flat (unfolded) sheet measures 210 × 180 mm.

How many cut and crease lines does box type M001 have?

The template contains 6 cut lines and 4 crease lines.

Can I change the dimensions of box type M001?

Yes. 3 parameters are adjustable, including l, w, f1. The die-cut outline is recalculated on every change.

Which export formats are supported?

SVG, DXF (R12), PDF and PNG. Cut and crease lines are kept as separate layers in SVG.
