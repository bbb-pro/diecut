Frequently asked questions

Can I use these dielines for production?
Yes, for sampling and die-making. Every template is drawn in millimetres with solid cut lines and dashed crease lines, and exports to DXF (R12 polylines), SVG, PDF at 1:1 and PNG. Before running a job, apply one compensation pass for the board thickness and the kerf of your cutting machine.
What is the difference between manufacturing, inner and outer size?
The manufacturing size is where the die line sits (the neutral layer). The inner size is the manufacturing size minus the inward offset on both sides; the outer size adds the outward offset. The thicker the board, the wider the gap — roughly 2 mm on 1 mm greyboard and 6 mm or more on 3 mm corrugated. Switching the reference only changes the readout, never the geometry.
Does the die line change when I edit a dimension?
Yes. Editing length, width, depth or any structural parameter re-solves the whole layout instead of scaling it: tuck flaps, dust flaps and fold angles are recomputed under the rules of that box type. Out-of-range values are clamped to the limit the box type allows and the clamped value is written back into the field.
Do I need an account or a licence?
No. All 1298 box types, dimension conversions and format exports are open without sign-up, with no export quota.
